## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Solo Leveling Launcher is a clean, distraction-free home screen built for people
who want to take back control of their time.

Instead of an endless grid of tempting icons, it puts your focus front and
center — track your screen time, set daily limits on the apps that pull you in,
and hard-block the ones you don't want to open at all.

LEVEL UP YOUR FOCUS
• See your real screen time, pickup count, and per-app usage at a glance.
• Set a daily time limit on any app — get a gentle focus check when you hit it.
• Hard-block distracting apps and adult-site domains so they simply won't open.
• A calm, minimalist home screen that keeps you intentional.

BUILT TO RESPECT YOU
• 100% on-device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data leaves your phone.
• Lightweight and fast.

HOW BLOCKING WORKS
Solo Leveling Launcher uses Android's Accessibility service only to notice when
a blocked app comes to the foreground, so it can show your focus reminder. It
never reads your screen content and never sends data anywhere.

Make every unlock count. Level up.
