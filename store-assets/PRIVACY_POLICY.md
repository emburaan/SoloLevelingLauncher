# Privacy Policy — Solo Leveling Launcher

**Last updated:** 2 June 2026

Solo Leveling Launcher ("the App", "we", "us") is a home-screen launcher and
digital-wellbeing app. This policy explains what the App accesses, why, and
how that information is handled.

## Summary

**The App does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.** Everything
the App needs to function is processed **on your device** and never leaves it.
There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no remote servers operated by
us.

## Information the App accesses on your device

The App requests the following permissions and system access **solely to
provide its features**. None of this information is uploaded anywhere.

| Permission / access | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| **Usage Access** (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`) | To show your screen-time, app pickup counts, and per-app daily usage so you can set and track focus limits. Read on-device only. |
| **Query installed apps** (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`) | A launcher must list and open the apps installed on your device. Used to display your app drawer and home screen. |
| **Accessibility Service** | To enforce app blocking and daily focus limits. When you enable a block, the service detects when a blocked app is opened and shows a focus reminder/overlay. It reads only the package name of the foreground app for this purpose. It does **not** read screen content, passwords, or personal text, and nothing is logged or transmitted. |
| **Display over other apps** (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`) | To show the focus-check overlay over a blocked app. |
| **Set as default home app** (`HOME` intent) | To act as your device launcher. |

## Data storage

All settings (your blocked apps, daily limits, and preferences) are stored
locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

## Data sharing

We do **not** share, sell, or transfer any information to third parties. The
App contains no third-party advertising or analytics services.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Accessibility Service disclosure

In compliance with Google Play policy: Solo Leveling Launcher uses the Android
Accessibility API **only** to detect when a user-selected blocked app moves to
the foreground, in order to display a focus reminder. This is the App's core
digital-wellbeing feature. The Accessibility Service does not collect or share
any user data and does not use the data for any purpose unrelated to app
blocking.
