Privacy Policy
Solo Leveling Launcher

Last updated: 2 June 2026

No data collected

Summary

The App does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything it needs to function is processed on your device and never leaves it. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no remote servers operated by us.

Information the App accesses on your device

The App requests the following permissions and system access solely to provide its features. None of this information is uploaded anywhere.

Permission / accessWhy it is used
Usage AccessTo show your screen-time, app pickup counts, and per-app daily usage so you can set and track focus limits. Read on-device only.
Query installed appsA launcher must list and open the apps installed on your device. Used to display your app drawer and home screen.
Accessibility ServiceTo enforce app blocking and daily focus limits. When you enable a block, the service detects when a blocked app is opened and shows a focus reminder. It reads only the package name of the foreground app for this purpose. It does not read screen content, passwords, or personal text, and nothing is logged or transmitted.
Display over other appsTo show the focus-check overlay over a blocked app.
Set as default home appTo act as your device launcher.

Data storage

All settings (your blocked apps, daily limits, and preferences) are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any information to third parties. The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Accessibility Service disclosure

In compliance with Google Play policy: Solo Leveling Launcher uses the Android Accessibility API only to detect when a user-selected blocked app moves to the foreground, in order to display a focus reminder. This is the App's core digital-wellbeing feature. The Accessibility Service does not collect or share any user data and does not use the data for any purpose unrelated to app blocking.

Changes to this policy
